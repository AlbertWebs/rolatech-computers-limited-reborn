# SEO migration

Observed public URL families include `/`, `/the-company`, `/contact-us`, `/center-of-excellence/{slug}`, `/laptops-for-hire`, `/macbook-for-hire`, `/tablets-for-hire`, `/projectors-for-hire`, `/terms-and-conditions`, `/privacy-policy`, `/blogs/{slug}`, `/e-commerce`, `/e-commerce/product/{slug}`, `/e-commerce/product/brand/{slug}`, `/e-commerce/product/tags/{slug}`, and `/e-commerce/search-results`.

The replacement preserves the observed public commerce, hire, company, and contact paths, and retains product quick-view URLs. A dynamic `/sitemap.xml` covers active products, categories, brands, and published articles plus primary pages. `robots.txt` leaves public content crawlable and excludes account/admin/checkout paths. Product details emit Product JSON-LD; query-filtered catalog results are noindex and canonicalized to their clean listing URL. Seven legacy blog posts were imported as escaped plain text. Before launch, crawl the legacy site, compare canonical URLs and metadata, create explicit 301 mappings for changed URLs, validate sitemap and robots rules, and test structured data against reconciled inventory. Legal and service content URLs remain pending migration and business review; no SEO equity or redirect completeness is claimed yet.
